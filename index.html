Intro
I’m a Product Designer currently leading design at Vêtir, with 5+ years of experience designing and shipping digital products end-to-end. I’ve designed across fashion, fintech, BFSI and SaaS – from consumer apps and websites to enterprise dashboards. I enjoy making complicated things feel simple but exciting, building systems that scale, and shaping products from the first brainstorming session to the final shipped interface.

When I’m not designing, you’ll find me planning my next trip, hunting for the best Vietnamese coffee, curating my personal foodie database on Google Maps, or vibe-coding the next app that’s going to solve one of my first-world problems.
